# Core entities

Type: grilling
Status: resolved

## Question

Beyond species records themselves, what should scientists be able to track?

## Answer

Two entities: Species (the taxonomic catalog entry — name, bioluminescence type, depth range, habitat; already modeled as `BioluminescentSpecies`) and Sighting (an individual field record: who logged it, where, when, at what depth, with photos and notes), with many Sightings per Species. See `CONTEXT.md` for the canonical definitions.
